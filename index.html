Parents call
From the parent app, a family taps once on arrival. Their child's name lights up on the right class board within a second.
Teachers dismiss
Every class has its own board. Yellow means called, grey means dismissed. One tap marks a student out — and one tap undoes it.
Built for AGS classes
KG1–KG3 sections by letter; Grades 1–12 with separate boys' and girls' sections — 7b1, 7g1 and so on.
Private by design
A parent can only ever see their own children. Teachers see only their school. Enforced in the database, not just the screen.
